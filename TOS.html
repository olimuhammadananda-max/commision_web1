Terms & Conditions

General

  • I will publish the commissioned artwork on my social media and portfolio (Private commission will be charged extra).

Payments

  • The price could change every time.
  • I apply the pay-first and pay-after-sketch method.

Revisions

  • Any major changes will incur additional charges — like the placement of poses, clothes, backgrounds or something that has to be completely redrawn.
  • If more than 3 revisions during sketching, additional fees will apply.
  • Color changes during staining can be done a maximum of 1 time.
  • If more than max revisions, additional price +$5/revision.

Deadlines & Delivery

  • It takes 14–21 days to finish your request (depending on your queue).
  • Additional deadline will be charged extra.

Commercial use
